Part 2: The Framework

5 Steps to Automate Any Workflow

This isn't about learning to code. It's about teaching AI to understand your workflow.

1

Define the Workflow

Write down exactly what you do, step by step. Be specific. AI thrives on clarity.

Example: Weekly Status Email
Every Friday:
• Check Zoho Books for completed orders
• Pull container tracking numbers
• Check if payments received
• Draft email to customer with updates
• Add personal note about any issues
• Send and log in system
Claude ChatGPT Google Doc
2

Break It Into Tasks

Which parts are pure data pulling? Which need your judgment? Separate them.

AI Can Handle:
✓ Pulling order data from system
✓ Checking tracking numbers
✓ Verifying payment status
✓ Drafting standard email format
You Handle:
• Reviewing for accuracy
• Adding context about delays
• Adjusting tone for VIP customers
• Final approval to send
3

Delegate to AI

Use tools like Claude, Zapier, or custom scripts. Tell AI exactly what to do and when.

The Prompt
"Every Friday at 3pm: Pull all orders from Zoho with status 'Shipped' from the past week. For each order, get tracking number and payment status. Draft an email to the customer with format: [template]. Flag any orders without tracking or payment."
Zapier Make.com Claude API
4

Watch It Happen

Let AI run once. Check everything. Did it work? What's missing? Refine the prompt.

First Run Reality
Expect 80% right, 20% needs tweaking. Maybe it missed partial shipments. Or formatted dates wrong. Or didn't know how to handle rush orders. That's normal - fix it iteratively.

This is the "flying the spaceship" phase - you're building while testing. A tweak here, a rebuild there. Either way, it paves the way.

5

Approve & Send

Now it's running. Every week, AI does the work. You review, approve, add your touch, send.

Your New Friday
3:00pm - AI finishes pulling data & drafting emails
3:05pm - You review 10 draft emails
3:10pm - Add personal notes to 2 VIP customers
3:12pm - Approve all, click send
3:15pm - Done. What used to take 2 hours took 15 minutes.

"Either way, it paves the way. Every tweak, every refactor, every adjustment - they're not just improvements. They're lessons. They're foundation. They're the path forward."
